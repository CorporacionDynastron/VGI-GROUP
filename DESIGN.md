## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elite, authoritative visual language tailored for high-tier civil engineering, institutional infrastructure, and premium corporate construction. The brand identity balances precision engineering with executive prestige: raw structural solidity paired with the refined luxury of brushed architectural metals.

### Aesthetic Movement & Character
The visual aesthetic fuses **High-End Corporate Minimalism** with **Technical Blueprint Constructivism**. It avoids fragile ornamentation in favor of crisp datum lines, technical grids, surveyor coordinates, and structural geometry. Every layout feels like a master architectural folio or executive technical blueprint executed with meticulous craftsmanship.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Audience:** Institutional investors, infrastructure developers, corporate stakeholders, municipal directors, and tier-one civil engineering partners.
- **Impression:** Absolute structural reliability, engineering exactness, uncompromised safety standards, and timeless corporate gravitas.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in deep structural darks punctuated by metallic brushed gold accents and high-legibility crisp light neutrals.

### Surface Hierarchy & Depth Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0D11`):** The deepest foundation layer, evoking structural obsidian and absolute precision.
- **Layer 01 Surface (`#11141A`):** Structural panels, full-width section alternating bands, and page frames.
- **Layer 02 Elevated Panels & Cards (`#1A1D24`):** Container background for project data cards, technical metric blocks, and interactive modals.
- **Layer 03 Interactive & Hover States (`#232832`):** Elevated tactile states, input backdrops, and active row indicators.

### Accent & Metallic Tokens
- **Primary Brushed Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Primary action items, seal borders, insignia highlights, and key brand anchors.
- **Warm Gold Highlight (`#E5C158`):** Glows, hover states, active metrics, and emphasis numerals.
- **Deep Burnished Gold (`#C59B27`):** Subdued technical rules, active outlines, and focused indicators.
- **Specular Gold / Pale Sheen (`#F3E5AB`):** High-light reflections, metallic gradients, and subtle badges.

### Typographic & Graphic Contrasts
- **Primary Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-impact structural headlines and hero statements.
- **Ivory Soft Text (`#F1F3F5`):** Long-form technical copy and primary body copy to reduce glare against dark substrates.
- **Platinum Grey (`#9CA3AF`):** Secondary metadata, datum lines, unit indicators (m², kN, LAT/LONG), and ISO certification text.
- **Hairline Border Chrome (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)` and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): 1px structural framing evoking blueprint layout guides.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces structural discipline:
1. **Headlines (`Syne`):** Imparts solid sculptural geometry with distinctive, structural cuts. Used with controlled tracking to communicate permanence and high-end engineering authority.
2. **Body & Prose (`Inter`):** Selected for uncompromised legibility, neutral technical clarity, and clean vertical metrics in multi-tier documentation.
3. **Technical Foliation & Coordinates (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to blueprint tags, sheet numbers (`HOJA 01 DE 03`), GPS coordinates, ISO standards, and project classification metadata. Always rendered in uppercase with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.12em` to `0.18em`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a **12-column rigid architectural grid** inspired by structural engineering blueprints.

### Structural Rhythm
- **Baseline Matrix:** Strict 8px baseline rhythm across all container heights and section padding.
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`1280px` max-width container) with `2.5rem` gutters. 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1024px`) with `1.5rem` gutters. 4-column layout on mobile (`<768px`) with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins.
- **Section Breaks:** Generous breathing room between macro-sections (`space-2xl` to `6rem`) framed with 1px architectural hairline division rules.
- **Micro Spacing:** Data chips and project spec points rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm` for dense, technical readouts without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through **tonal stratification and architectural metallic hairlines** rather than standard diffuse drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation Ground):** `#0B0D11`, completely flat, absorptive.
- **Tier 1 (Sub-Panels & Structural Modules):** `#11141A` bounded by a 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Engineering Cards):** `#1A1D24` layered over `#11141A` with a dual-accent edge: 1px top border tinted with gold sheen (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`) and bottom/side edges in `#232832`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Overlays):** `#1A1D24` backdrop with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an ambient shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

### Lighting & Metallic Accents
- **Brushed Gold Shimmer:** A fine linear gradient (`135deg, #C59B27 0%, #F3E5AB 50%, #D4AF37 100%`) applied selectively to active indicator badges, key CTA borders, and circular seal emblems.
- **Glow Accents:** Soft, focused gold ambient halos (`0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`) reserved exclusively for primary interactive states and verified structural certifications.

## Shapes

The shape system strictly uses **Sharp Geometry (`roundedness: 0`)** with surgical chamfered cuts on technical badges and metadata tags.

- **Containers & Cards:** 0px radius (rectilinear corners). Surfaces reflect precision-cut stone, structural steel plates, and technical drafting sheets.
- **Technical Badges & Spec Chips:** 0px radius with optional 45-degree chamfered top-right corners (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) to mirror architectural project drawings and field tag stamps.
- **Circular Elements:** Reserved strictly for corporate seals, ISO badges, metric circular progress rings, and round insignia emblems.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Bars
- **Primary Executive CTA:** Solid brushed gold background (`#D4AF37` fading subtly to `#C59B27`), text in `#0B0D11` bold uppercase `JetBrains Mono` (`label-caps`), 0px corner radius, padding `16px 28px`. Hover shifts to `#E5C158` with a subtle technical box-shadow `0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary Technical Button:** `#1A1D24` background, 1px solid `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` border, text in `#F1F3F5`. Hover triggers full-frame gold border illumination (`#D4AF37`) with text tinting to `#F3E5AB`.
- **Text & Folio Links:** Pure gold inline label followed by an architectural arrow symbol (`→` or `+`), underlined by a 1px dashed hairline that solidifies on hover.

### Technical Cards & Dossiers
- **Project & Service Cards:** Framed by 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. The header contains technical foliation (e.g., `HOJA 01 DE 03 // OBRA-CIVIL`) in `JetBrains Mono` uppercase with platinum gray metadata.
- **Image Treatment:** Architectural project photography carries a high-contrast industrial tone with subtle warm desaturation. On hover, the image scales minimally (`1.02`) under a fine dark gradient scrim.

### Metric Indicators & Seal Rings
- **Stats Blocks:** Giant numerals rendered in `Syne` Bold with subtle gold tone (`#E5C158`), accompanied by a micro label in platinum gray and an optional circular hairline ring with a subtle gold perimeter arc.
- **Accreditation Badges:** ISO and institutional engineering stamps inverted into monochrome light platinum (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), turning to brushed gold tint on hover.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Text Inputs:** Dark graphite fill (`#11141A`), bottom border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`. Focus state elevates the bottom border to 2px solid `#D4AF37` with a subtle golden glow.
- **Labels:** Floated above in uppercase `JetBrains Mono` with small platinum indicators.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Switches
- **Form Selectors:** Square architectural checkboxes (0px radius) with `#1A1D24` background and 1px `#D4AF37` border when checked. Checkmark rendered as an architectural crosshair or solid gold micro-square.
